# Alexander Zaroson

Custode dell'Aria

## Benedizione di Stor: 

As a bonus action on your turn you can activate the following bonuses for 1 hour: 

- You have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage. 

- You have resistance to fire damage. 

- You are considered of one size larger. 

- Your weapons are considered of one size larger and deal 1d8 of extra damage. 

- Your hp maximum is increased by 25. 

- You ignore resistances to fire damage and treat immunities as resistances
