# Howl

WHAT DOES HE WANT: 

He wants to escape from his past and forget or overcome his fears while not neglecting his duties. 

WHAT DOES HE FEAR: 

The Shadowfell and his inhabitants, nightmares of his past griefs. 

CONTEXT: 

Howl was born into a rich and noble family of Klinthur. They were famous merchants of wovens and clothes, know all over the country. Less known was their large involvement in the cult of Death, and, more particularly, in a specific tradition focused on blood called “Crimson Death”. Howl’s father was also the Keeper of the Death cult, therefore had to attend numerous duty across the country in Urbe. 

Howl was educated by various instructor and teachers on all kinds of matter both cultural and practical. 

He was also taught the way of the sword by precise indications of his father, who probably already knew that he would’ve probably had to take his place one day in the future. That was actually the case, one day his father had to attend a highly physically taxing ceremony, which involved the lost of a great amount of blood; due to this significant weakening he fell sick and his debilitated body contracted a rare disease which led him to a quick death. The healers didn’t even have the time to act, due also to the fact that those practice weren’t publicly accepted and therefore, done in private circumstances. 

Howl was rapidly pointed as the appropriate successor to his father, so he had to face the initiation, which is a ritual where the candidate must pass a trial and come out stronger, wiser and reborn. 

The trial is unique for everyone and Howl ended up with a journey in the plain of Shadowfell, where he faced numerous entities and came back with a trophy. 

He succeeded in his mission and claimed his new title, becoming the 394th Keeper of Death. 

Not too long after his designation, his mother got assassinated by what seemed to be professional assassins. They burst into their house in Rastoff while Howl wasn’t in the city and murdered her with several knife stabs. 

Howl started to track them down immediately after burying his mother, learning that the assassins where part of an organization called “The Black Hand”, hailing from Havbukt. To locate every member of the organization involved in his mother murder, he managed to find a powerful ally, even though he didn’t realize how much powerful until he finished his personal business with The Black Hand. 

When the last accessory was killed, he turned to his helper to thank him; only at that point this generous fellow revealed himself as the Hidden King. He had heard about the new Keeper of Death to be as skillful as his predecessor, but he wanted to test him by himself and, if satisfied, offer him to become one of his best man. 

Howl initially refused the offer, but as he grew bored of his occupations, went back to Havbukt and accepted the place at the king's side. 

Growing bored is actually what he says to the people who ask him the reason of that change of mind, or at least the ones that he bothers to answer. The truth is that even though the family’s business has been conducted by his parent’s associate, he still has nightmares about his griefs and everything he did or saw in his house or during his duty as a Keeper, and he needed some distraction to not go crazy.
